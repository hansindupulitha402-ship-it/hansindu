PayHere setup required

The checkout is ready for PayHere, but real online payments should not be activated until your merchant account and server-side credentials are configured.

Why?
PayHere requires a Merchant ID and a Merchant Secret, and payment notifications must be verified on a publicly accessible server. The secret must never be placed in browser JavaScript.

What you need

  1. Create/approve your PayHere merchant account.
  2. Add your website domain under PayHere Integrations.
  3. Put the Merchant ID and Merchant Secret in the server-side configuration.
  4. Set the payment notification URL on your live domain.
